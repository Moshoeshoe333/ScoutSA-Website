# ScoutSA public positioning

**Status:** Foundation proposal  
**Audience:** Website contributors and reviewers  
**Disclosure level:** Public-safe

## 1. Purpose

The ScoutSA website is an outward-facing introduction and relationship-building channel. Its primary purpose is to attract credible collaborators, establish trust in the mission, explain the problem space at an appropriate level, and invite thoughtful conversations.

It is not intended to function as a product manual, technical specification, recruitment portal, player database, or proof that future platform capabilities are operational.

## 2. Public proposition

**ScoutSA — Discover. Verify. Connect.**

ScoutSA is an initiative exploring how grassroots football talent and credible football opportunities can be connected more responsibly in South Africa.

The public story should communicate:
- the importance of making grassroots talent more discoverable;
- the need for trustworthy relationships and responsible opportunity pathways;
- the value of collaboration among players' support networks, coaches, academies, football organisations, technology contributors and responsible partners;
- a commitment to safeguarding, privacy, transparency and realistic claims.

Use exploratory language where work is still being researched or developed: "aims to", "is exploring", "is being designed to", and "seeks to". Do not imply that a capability exists merely because it is planned.

## 3. Primary audiences

1. **Football practitioners:** coaches, academies, development programmes and relevant football organisations.
2. **Safeguarding and community stakeholders:** guardians, educators, community organisations and child-protection practitioners.
3. **Technical collaborators:** software, data, design, security and product professionals.
4. **Strategic partners:** organisations that may contribute expertise, access, facilities, funding or responsible support.
5. **Supporters and observers:** people interested in responsible grassroots football development.

## 4. Desired visitor journey

1. Understand the mission in under a minute.
2. Recognise the real-world problem and why it matters.
3. See the principles that guide the initiative.
4. Understand that the work is developing and that no outcomes are guaranteed.
5. Find a clear, low-pressure route to express interest in collaboration.

The desired conversion is a qualified conversation, not an account registration.

## 5. Tone and visual identity

Adopt the approved A+B direction: premium football presentation combined with authentic grassroots warmth.

- Confident, clear and human; never boastful.
- Professional without feeling inaccessible.
- South African in context without relying on stereotypes.
- Strong editorial hierarchy, accessible contrast and mobile-first layouts.
- Use authentic, appropriately licensed imagery. Do not use identifiable minors without documented permission and a valid safeguarding basis.

## 6. Calls to action

Preferred calls to action:
- Explore the mission
- Our approach
- Collaborate with ScoutSA
- Start a conversation
- Express interest

Avoid:
- Register as a player
- Get discovered now
- Guaranteed trials
- Find an agent
- Apply for placement

Those imply operational services or outcomes that are not approved for the initial public website.

## 7. Success measures

Early website success should be assessed through:
- relevant collaboration enquiries;
- quality and diversity of stakeholder conversations;
- useful feedback from football and safeguarding practitioners;
- accessibility, reliability and content accuracy.

Traffic volume alone is not evidence of trust, impact or product-market fit.
