## Contact interaction and technical boundary

- The prototype may include a visible email link that opens the visitor's configured email application.
- A name/email/message form is not a functioning submission channel unless a delivery provider, processing owner, privacy notice, retention rule and abuse controls have been reviewed and configured.
- Do not present a static form as if messages are being delivered.
- Do not collect player profiles, identity documents, dates of birth, guardian details, medical information, location data or other sensitive information through this contact section.
- Do not invite minors to send private personal information. The copy should direct players and families to communicate with an appropriate parent/guardian involved.
- No automated registration, trial booking, opportunity application or player submission is implied.

## Review conditions before launch

1. Confirm that the mailbox is monitored and identify who is responsible for responding.
2. Decide what categories of enquiries are accepted and how they are triaged.
3. Publish a proportionate privacy notice before adding any form or collecting personal information.
4. Establish retention, access, deletion and incident-handling procedures for incoming messages.
5. Reassess safeguarding language before inviting enquiries from under-18 players.

## Acceptance criteria for the prototype

- The section is labelled **Connect With ScoutSA**.
- The supplied email address is displayed accurately and opens a `mailto:` action.
- No unverified phone, address, hours, registration or match-day claims appear.
- The prototype makes no claim that a form submits successfully.
- The section fits the approved Direction AB visual language and is usable on mobile and desktop.
